What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measurement that uses a person's weight and height to estimate whether their weight falls within a general healthy range. It is commonly used as a screening measure for adults.

BMI Categories for Adults

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or higher Obesity

BMI is a screening tool and does not directly measure body fat. Factors such as muscle mass, age and other health characteristics should also be considered.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in metres squared.

Is BMI the same for men and women?

The standard adult BMI categories are generally applied to both men and women, although BMI does not account for differences in body composition.

Can children use this calculator?

Children and teenagers require age- and sex-specific BMI interpretation. This calculator is designed for adults.
