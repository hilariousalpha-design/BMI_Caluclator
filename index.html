BMI Guide

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple calculation that compares a person's weight with their height. It is commonly used as a screening measure to place adults into broad weight categories.

How is BMI calculated?

For metric measurements, BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in metres squared.

What does my BMI mean?

BMI can provide a general indication of whether your weight falls within a commonly used range. However, BMI does not directly measure body fat and does not account for factors such as muscle mass, body composition, age or individual health circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly classified as the healthy-weight range.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI is a screening tool and does not directly measure body fat or body composition. It may not accurately reflect health status for everyone.
