Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a calculation based on a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a screening measure to classify weight status.


Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening tool and does not directly measure body fat or overall health. A healthcare professional can provide a more complete assessment.
